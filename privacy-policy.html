Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

At Two Stars Engineering & Construction Company Pvt. Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our"), accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by us and how we use it.

This policy is compliant with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us through our "Contact Us" form. This information includes:

  • Personal Identification Information: Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Project Information: Details about the service you are interested in and the message/scope of work you provide.

We do not collect any Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) such as passwords, financial information, health data, or biometric data.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We do not use cookies for tracking purposes. We do not employ any third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or behavioral advertising technologies on our website. Your browsing activity on this site is completely private.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide through the contact form is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide estimated quotes.
  • To communicate with you regarding your specific project requirements.
  • To maintain internal records of business communications.

We will not use your information for marketing purposes unless you explicitly consent to it.

4. Third-Party Data Processing

Our contact form is processed using a third-party service called FormSubmit. When you submit the form, your data is routed through their servers to reach our email inbox. We recommend reviewing FormSubmit's privacy policy to understand how they handle data in transit. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any other third parties.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure, as required by Indian law. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights (Under DPDP Act, 2023)

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Withdraw consent for us to process your data.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
